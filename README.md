A complete flight game that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files. Fly the Skyharbour Valley in one of 6 aircraft — Kestrel S-2, Merlin M-4, Albatross A-9, Falcon F-7, Heron H-3, Condor C-12 — across 8 airfields (Harbour Field, Mill Crossing, Lakeside Strip, North Pass, Quarry Flat, South Ridge, Gull Island, Observatory Top) and 7 missions plus Free Flight: First Solo, Ring Run, Mountain Rescue, Mail Run, Storm Crossing, Island Hop, and the Grand Tour.

Chase, cockpit, tower, and cinematic cameras; a lit 3D cockpit dashboard with a live artificial horizon, airspeed, altimeter, and heading dials; a HUD with altitude, speed, and heading; live traffic AI (aircraft, gliders, flocks); weather and wind; engine and wind audio; PAPI lights, threshold bars, and approach lighting on every strip. Keyboard and mouse, no network requests, deterministic flight model.
